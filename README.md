# Sales Revenue, Profit Margin & Monthly Growth Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales revenue, estimated profit, profit margins, and monthly growth using Python and Microsoft Power BI. It demonstrates data preparation, exploratory data analysis (EDA), data visualization, and business intelligence reporting.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales revenue and estimated profit.
- Compare product-wise and region-wise performance.
- Examine category-wise revenue distribution.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends and growth.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Microsoft Power BI
- Git and GitHub
- CSV Dataset

## Project Structure
- `Dataset/` – Extended sales dataset and dataset documentation.
- `Python Files/` – Python data preparation and EDA scripts.
- `Visualizations/` – Five generated data visualizations.
- `Power BI Files/` – Interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Revenue: ₹70,68,000
- Total Estimated Profit: Approximately ₹14 lakh
- Average Estimated Profit Margin: 25.15%

## Visualizations
1. Product-wise Total Revenue
2. Region-wise Revenue
3. Category-wise Revenue Distribution
4. Monthly Revenue Trend
5. Average Profit Margin by Product

## Business Insights
1. Laptops contribute the highest revenue among the products.
2. The North region generates the highest revenue.
3. Electronics contributes the largest share of revenue.
4. Monthly revenue fluctuates significantly across the analyzed period.
5. Headphones have the highest estimated profit margin under the assumed cost structure.

## Recommendations
1. Focus on high-revenue products while maintaining sufficient inventory.
2. Review lower-revenue months and plan targeted marketing campaigns.

## Dataset Limitations
The dataset contains 20 illustrative sales records. Dates were added for monthly analysis, and product cost rates are assumed for estimating profit and profit margins. These figures are not actual company financial records.
